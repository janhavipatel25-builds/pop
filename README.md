# Pop 🫧

> A Mac break timer that makes pausing a little joyful.

**Status:** Phase 0 — planning. Nothing to download yet.

## What it is
Pop lives in your Mac menu bar. Pick 20, 40 or 60 minutes. When time's up, a bubble forms on your screen and grows for 5 seconds. It stays until you pop it. Pop it, and you get a small prompt to rest (like "Find 5 things 20 feet away").

## Why
Most break timers are forgettable or annoying, so people snooze them. In Pop, the reminder *is* the fun part: you play with it instead of dismissing it.

## Origin story
Pop started as a physical bubble timer that Jahnavi Patel and Abhiram Rajilan designed for the IDHA "Future By Design" competition (2019, awarded). This is the digital version, built and shipped.

## How it's built (planned)
- One bubble component, two shells: a web demo (GitHub Pages) and a Mac menu-bar app (Tauri).
- React + Vite + Tailwind.
